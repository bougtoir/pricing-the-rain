# Phase 6 report: ecological-economics positioning recheck

## Verdict

**GO WITH NARROWED CLAIMS.** The realized contribution fits *Ecological Economics*
when framed as an analysis of how environmental scarcity is translated into demand,
selection, valuation, expenditure, and reputation under hidden counterfactuals. It is
not a paper claiming new evidence about historical ritual prices or a universal
dry-spell hazard.

## Contribution after seeing results

1. Empirical hazards are estimated rather than assumed and are non-monotonic across
   dry-spell bins in all eight stress-test locations.
2. Actual rainfall yields substantial apparent success under exact zero efficacy.
3. Drought demand and provider selection have distinct effects: demand changes event
   frequency, while selection changes observed performance.
4. Belief and memory generate price premia and provider differentiation without causal
   environmental benefit.
5. Price premia do not guarantee a positive event-level price–outcome association.
6. Monetary WTP, transfers, causal rainfall value, and non-monetary social value are
   explicitly separated.

## Closest-work boundary

Espín-Sánchez, Gil-Guirado, and Ryan establish the persuasive role of endogenous
rain-prayer timing and rainfall hazards. This manuscript does not claim that insight.
Its incremental domain is the coupled ecological-economic system of scarcity-driven
demand, strategic provider acceptance, plural valuation, pricing, imperfect
counterfactual attribution, and reputation.

## Desk-reject risks after results

- **Insufficient empirical identification:** price is simulated. The manuscript must
  present the real-weather component as a mechanism stress test and the pricing results
  as calibrated theory.
- **Overextended novelty:** the rainfall-timing mechanism is prior work. The abstract
  and introduction must lead with selection, valuation, and reputation.
- **Unsupported positive-price result:** the clustered interval includes zero and the
  stricter permutation and M4–M5 associations are negative. This is a boundary result,
  not a result to hide.
- **Cultural reductionism:** plural and relational values must remain distinct from WTP.
- **Limited geographic representation:** eight points span regimes but do not estimate
  global or community-specific effects.
- **Model arbitrariness:** parameter sensitivity, convergence, complete code, and
  machine-readable values must accompany every quantitative claim.

## Submission route

Retain the **Analysis** article type. A Methodological and Ideological Options framing
would overstate the methodological novelty, while a pure empirical route would
overstate identification. The target remains defensible if the title, abstract, and
conclusion consistently use “apparent success,” “scenario price,” and “zero
meteorological efficacy.”
