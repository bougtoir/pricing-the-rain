# Formal ecological-economic model

## 1. Potential rainfall and the zero-effect invariant

For region \(r\) and day \(t\), let \(R_{rt}(a)\) denote precipitation under
pseudo-intervention state \(a\in\{0,1\}\). The defining structural restriction is

\[
R_{rt}(1)=R_{rt}(0)=R_{rt}.
\]

No request, acceptance, price, provider, belief, or reputation variable enters the
meteorological equation. All estimated “success” is therefore apparent success.

For rainfall threshold \(c\) and window \(k\), define the future natural-rain indicator

\[
Y_{rt}(k,c)=\mathbb{1}\left(\max_{\tau=1,\ldots,k}R_{r,t+\tau}\geq c\right).
\]

The prospective baseline hazard is

\[
q_{rt}(k,c)=
\Pr\{Y_{rt}(k,c)=1\mid r,s_t,D_{rt},L_{rt},\mathcal I_t\},
\]

where \(s_t\) is season, \(D_{rt}\) is drought state, \(L_{rt}\) is preceding dry-spell
duration, and \(\mathcal I_t\) contains only information available by day \(t\). No
monotonicity in \(L_{rt}\) is imposed.

## 2. Environmental scarcity and requests

Let \(E_{rt}\geq0\) be environmental/economic exposure to continued dryness and
\(U^{S}_{irt}\geq0\) be non-meteorological social or ritual utility for household or
community \(i\). A request is generated by

\[
\Pr(A^{req}_{irt}=1)=
\operatorname{logit}^{-1}
\left(\beta_{0r}+\beta_DD_{rt}+\beta_EE_{rt}
-\beta_BB_{irt}+\beta_SU^{S}_{irt}\right),
\]

where \(B_{irt}\) denotes resource constraints. In the empirical pseudo-event analysis,
request rules are deterministic pre-specified timing rules rather than estimated
historical behavior.

## 3. Providers and selective acceptance

Provider \(j\) has selectivity intercept \(\xi_j\). In M0-M2, a requested event is
accepted with fixed probability 0.85. In M3-M5, acceptance is

\[
\Pr(A^{acc}_{jrt}=1)=
\operatorname{logit}^{-1}
\left(\gamma_0+\gamma_q(q_{rt}-0.5)+\xi_j\right).
\]

The implemented acceptance rule uses the prospective hazard and provider selectivity;
price and reputation do not enter acceptance. This is a calibrated theoretical
strategy, not an estimate of historical provider behavior. It changes which natural
outcomes become observed and never affects rainfall.

## 4. Apparent and perceived efficacy

For accepted pseudo-event \(e=(j,r,t)\),

\[
S_e(k,c)=A^{acc}_{jrt}Y_{rt}(k,c).
\]

A naive Beta-Binomial observer maintains

\[
\theta_{jt}\mid\mathcal H_{jt}\sim
\operatorname{Beta}(a_0+s_{jt},b_0+n_{jt}-s_{jt}),
\]

and reports perceived incremental efficacy

\[
\widehat\delta^{N}_{jrt}=
\mathbb E(\theta_{jt}\mid\mathcal H_{jt})-\bar q_r.
\]

A meteorologically informed observer subtracts the event-specific counterfactual:

\[
\widehat\delta^{M}_{jrt}=
\mathbb E(\theta_{jt}\mid\mathcal H_{jt})-q_{rt}.
\]

A recency observer replaces counts by exponentially discounted counts with
\(w_h=\lambda^h\). A selective-memory observer uses success weight
\(w_+>w_-\), making remembered success frequency

\[
\tilde\theta_{jt}=
\frac{a_0+w_+s_{jt}}
{a_0+b_0+w_+s_{jt}+w_-(n_{jt}-s_{jt})}.
\]

These are stylized attribution rules, not claims about any historical population.

## 5. Plural value, WTP, and prices

Let \(V^R_{irt}\) denote the marginal instrumental value of natural rainfall or avoided
drought loss. Let \(U^S_{irt}\) denote separable social, psychological, coordination,
solidarity, or ritual utility. In the implemented M4-M5 scenarios,
provider reputation is compared with the current prospective hazard:

\[
\operatorname{WTP}_{irt}=
B_{irt}\left[
U^S_{irt}+V^R_{irt}\max(0,\rho_{jt}-q_{rt})
\right],
\qquad p_{jrt}=0.8\,\operatorname{WTP}_{irt},
\]

where \(B_{irt}\in[0,1]\) captures ability to pay. This expression is a scenario model,
not an assertion that cultural values are fully commensurable. M0-M3 instead use the
fixed scenario price specified in the configuration.

The following quantities remain distinct:

1. **Actuarially fair causal rainfall price:** \(p^{causal}=V^R\delta=0\), because
   \(\delta=0\).
2. **Belief-mediated expected-value price:** \(p^{belief}=V^R\widehat\delta\).
3. **WTP or market-clearing ceiling:** \(p^{wtp}=\operatorname{WTP}\).
4. **Theoretical revenue-maximizing provider price:**
   \(p^{rev}=\arg\max_p p\,\Pr(A^{req}=1,\operatorname{WTP}\ge p)\).
5. **Welfare-neutral payment:** a transfer that leaves modeled material-plus-social
   utility unchanged, conditional on the explicitly chosen welfare boundary.

Positive observed prices under the model can arise from beliefs, social utility,
information asymmetry, or coordination value, but never from true rain generation.
The simulation does not solve for \(p^{rev}\); it uses the transparent \(0.8\) WTP
pricing rule above.

## 6. Reputation

In M4, provider \(j\) begins with Beta prior \((a_0,b_0)\), and the reputation stock
available before event \(t\) is

\[
\rho_{jt}=
\frac{a_0+\sum_{\tau<t}S_{j\tau}}
{a_0+b_0+\sum_{\tau<t}A^{acc}_{j\tau}}.
\]

In M5, a log-odds memory stock follows

\[
m_{j,t+1}=\lambda m_{jt}+
\begin{cases}
w_+,&S_{jt}=1,\\
-w_-,&S_{jt}=0,
\end{cases}
\qquad
\rho_{jt}=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}(m_{jt}).
\]

Current apparent success remains \(S_{jrt}=Y_{rt}\), a flow generated by current
natural rainfall. Current price depends on the lagged reputation stock and current
hazard. This timing permits reputation-mediated price premia and provider-price
heterogeneity without a positive contemporaneous price-success association.

Community expenditure and provider revenue are the same modeled transfer. Provider
resource cost is \(c n_j\), provider profit is revenue minus that cost, and total social
surplus excludes the transfer and equals modeled social utility minus provider resource
cost. These are scenario accounts, not historical welfare estimates.

## 7. Estimands

The primary estimands are:

- apparent success by timing rule, region, threshold, and window;
- apparent-success differences across the staged M0-M5 comparison;
- naive and hazard-adjusted price–success associations;
- scenario WTP and simulated price distributions;
- provider-price and reputation dispersion;
- transfer, resource-cost, profit, and surplus accounts;
- the change in each quantity from M0 through M5.

The theoretical revenue-maximizing price is distinguished conceptually but not estimated.
No estimand is interpreted as historical ritual efficacy or historical payment.
