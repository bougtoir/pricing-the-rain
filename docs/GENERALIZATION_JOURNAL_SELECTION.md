## Decision

- **Primary: Economic Modelling (Elsevier).** JCR Q1 Economics; hybrid — no
  mandatory APC (subscription route); publishes theory-plus-simulation papers
  with behavioral mechanisms. Required reframing: lead with the formal
  four-layer pricing model and comparative-statics/simulation results;
  present rainfall as a validation environment in one dedicated section.
- **Backup: Journal of Risk and Uncertainty (Springer).** Best subject fit
  (rare events, probability weighting, insurance demand); hybrid Open Choice —
  no mandatory APC; Scopus Q1 / ABDC A*; JCR Economics quartile ~Q2, so held
  as backup if the Q1 constraint binds on primary.

## Format implications for the package (Economic Modelling)

- Article type: full-length research article; no strict word cap in guide for
  authors (keep ~8,000–10,000 words); abstract ~≤200 words; highlights +
  JEL codes required at submission (title page lists JEL D81, D84, D91, C63).
- Reference style: Elsevier author–date (APA-compatible); existing APA
  machinery carries over.
- Figures/tables: embedded or separate acceptable; keep separate files + in-
  text placement consistent with prior package.
- Data/code: data availability statement required — point to the public repo
  + archived raw inputs ledger.
- Declaration of interest + author placeholders stay bracketed.
