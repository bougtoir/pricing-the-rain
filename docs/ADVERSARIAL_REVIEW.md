# Hostile-but-fair reviewer and editor review

## Editorial verdict

**MAJOR REFRAMING, THEN GO.** The exact-zero design and reproducible implementation are
coherent, but the baseline manuscript understates its overlap with Espín-Sánchez,
Gil-Guirado, and Ryan (2026). A defensible *Ecological Economics* Analysis must center
on selective provider performance, provider-specific reputation-mediated pricing, and
the failure of price to predict current apparent success. Endogenous timing, increasing
hazards, scarcity-dependent demand, generic benefit-cost reasoning, and persistence of
persuasive practices are prior territory.

## Hostile objections and required repairs

### 1. “This is *Praying for Rain* plus a simulation.”

This objection is valid against the baseline framing. The QJE article already has zero
rainmaking efficacy, endogenous drought timing, increasing rainfall hazards, hidden
counterfactual persuasion, demand for rain, tangible benefits and support costs,
donations and payments in historical context, cultural selection, leader persistence,
and an explicit strategic-timing alternative. The manuscript must not claim those
ingredients as its novelty.

**Repair:** treat QJE as the foundation. Define the increment as a request-to-acceptance
provider system with rejected requests, multiple providers, provider-specific
reputation stocks, endogenous scenario prices, selective memory, explicit accounting,
and risk-adjusted performance. Make the negative contemporaneous price-success result
the result that distinguishes the extension.

### 2. “Rainmaking is a gimmick.”

The application will look exotic and narrow if ritual is the object rather than the
stress test. The eight locations do not identify historical communities, and the model
does not estimate actual ritual efficacy.

**Repair:** lead with a general evaluation problem: environmental providers act when
natural hazards are changing, select exposure, and are judged without the untreated
counterfactual. Use fictional rainfall events as a transparent limiting case. Retain
respectful historical context without presenting cultures as irrational.

### 3. “Scenario WTP is not empirical environmental valuation.”

Correct. The WTP regressor is constructed and the price model is calibrated. Calling
this “environmental valuation” in the title overstates the empirical content.

**Repair:** remove “Environmental Valuation” from the title; use “scenario WTP,”
“simulated endogenous price,” or “calibrated pricing mechanism” everywhere applicable.
State that no historical WTP, price schedule, or currency amount is estimated.

### 4. “The positive price hypothesis failed.”

Correct, and treating this as a side note would look like outcome switching. The
two-way clustered interval includes zero, the within-stratum estimate is negative, and
M4-M5 price-success differences are negative.

**Repair:** elevate failure of the positive association to the abstract, principal
results, Figure 4, discussion, conclusion, highlights, and cover letter. Do not retune
parameters. Recast the contribution as price/reputation decoupling rather than a
positive price-performance claim.

### 5. “The stock-flow explanation is post hoc.”

The explanation is supported by the model equations but is not yet sufficiently visible.
Reputation is updated only from past attributed outcomes; current price depends on that
stock relative to current hazard; current success is natural rainfall governed by the
current ecological state. A high reputation can therefore support a premium on a
low-hazard day, generating a negative contemporaneous difference.

**Repair:** write the timing explicitly in the formal model and manuscript, and redesign
Figure 4 to show mean prices and reputation dispersion alongside the negative
price(success)-price(failure) difference. Present this as a model mechanism, not an
empirical historical law.

### 6. “The eight sites are arbitrary.”

They are not a probability sample and are not matched to ritual communities. Even if
pre-specified to span rainfall regimes, they cannot establish global prevalence,
historical transportability, or representative external validity.

**Repair:** call them pre-specified mechanism stress tests. State the selection logic
and limitations. Do not compare their evidentiary breadth favorably with the QJE
cross-cultural sample.

### 7. “M0-M5 is not a causal decomposition.”

Correct. M0-M1 shuffle outcomes within sampled years, while M2-M5 restore observed
local and seasonal ordering. Adjacent differences can alter more than one statistical
property and cannot automatically be read as one-factor causal effects.

**Repair:** use “staged model comparison,” “sequential generative enrichment,” or
“mechanism decomposition.” State exactly what is shuffled, restored, or added at each
stage. Reserve causal language for the imposed zero rainfall effect and controlled
within-model mechanisms.

### 8. “Provider strategy is theoretical.”

Correct. Historical sources establish practitioners, requests, collections, offerings,
and context-specific payment, but they do not identify the calibrated acceptance slope
or a universal hazard-screening strategy.

**Repair:** label strategic acceptance as a theoretical possibility result using
observed weather. Do not claim that historical providers used the modeled forecasts.

### 9. “The permutation result is overinterpreted.”

The \(p \approx 0.002\) result comes from a constructed scenario-WTP regressor, overlapping
event windows, selected strata, and an exploratory algorithm. It is not a confirmatory
test of a historical market hypothesis.

**Repair:** describe it as a within-stratum falsification diagnostic. Report the
algorithm, 1,000 permutations, exchangeability assumption, and negative sign, but do
not use the p-value as proof of a general inverse relationship.

### 10. “Cultural practices are reduced to monetary utility.”

The risk remains whenever historical ritual is connected to price or WTP. A separate
social-utility term does not empirically measure cultural, relational, political, or
spiritual meaning.

**Repair:** distinguish historical plausibility from calibration and estimation.
Maintain the explicit inequality among environmental-service value,
intervention-created value, scenario WTP, price, transfers, resource costs, and social
value. State that the model neither exhausts nor monetizes cultural meaning.

### 11. “The ecological-economics contribution is decorative.”

The paper would fail this test if rainfall were only a colorful random process. The
ecological contribution is that local and seasonal hazard defines the opportunity set
for requests, selection, attribution, and observed performance. The economic
contribution is the separation of value, price, transfer, cost, rent, and plural value
under hidden counterfactuals.

**Repair:** connect the results to risk-adjusted environmental-provider performance,
information asymmetry, scarcity-dependent demand, plural valuation, and institutional
feedback. Avoid generic behavioral-economics language with rainfall added afterward.

### 12. “The reproducibility claim exceeds the supplied package.”

The claim is only defensible if the public repository contains all code and
redistributable analytical inputs, exact acquisition instructions exist for excluded
sources, the public branch matches the canonical release after exclusions, and a clean
pinned-environment reproduction regenerates values and documents.

**Repair:** run the clean reproduction after all edits; audit secrets, licenses, large
files, manifests, and public exclusions; and describe restricted literature and
publisher snapshots as private audit material rather than public inputs.

### 13. “Simulation precision is being mistaken for realism.”

Ten thousand replications reduce Monte Carlo error but do not validate provider
behavior, observer psychology, WTP calibration, historical applicability, or external
validity.

**Repair:** keep percentile intervals explicitly labeled as Monte Carlo distributions,
not confidence intervals for historical parameters, and keep all calibration-dependent
claims conditional.

### 14. “The zero-effect null predetermines the answer.”

The null is an intentional stress test, not an estimate of actual rainmaking or other
environmental interventions. It establishes what institutional patterns are possible
without intervention-created rainfall.

**Repair:** state that exact zero efficacy is imposed, not discovered, and that the
design cannot support a claim that real interventions or rituals are ineffective.

## Acceptance gate

Proceed only after the title, abstract, contribution paragraph, Figure 4, M0-M5
language, cover letter, highlights, literature audit, and final report all implement the
narrowed contribution. Preserve the unfavorable price result and all author-action
placeholders.
