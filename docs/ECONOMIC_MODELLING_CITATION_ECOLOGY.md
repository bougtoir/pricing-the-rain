# Economic Modelling citation ecology audit

Date: 2026-10-15. Scope: positioning of the manuscript as a model-first,
comparative-statics paper for Economic Modelling.

## Positioning verdict

The manuscript reads as a model + comparative-statics paper with a
controlled benchmark, not as a rainmaking paper, a behavioral essay, or an
empirical pricing paper. Model sections (2–4) precede the benchmark (5),
propositions P1–P6 carry explicit evidentiary classification (Table 1), and
all simulation/benchmark outputs are labeled parameterized scenarios.

## Citation ecology by cluster

- **Endogenous selection / case-mix**: Heckman (1979); surgeon report-card
  selection literature (Shahian et al. 2011; Werner & Asch 2005; Barnett et
  al. 2005); Dranove & Satterthwaite (2000) on provider heterogeneity.
- **Misspecified / endogenous learning**: Esponda & Pouzo (2016, Berk–Nash);
  Jehiel (2018, analogy-based expectations); Esponda & Vespa (2018,
  endogenous sample selection in the lab); Bikhchandani et al. (1992,
  cascades); **added this pass: Shea (2015, Economic Modelling)** — bounded
  learning under endogenous variables can degrade inference, an in-journal
  precedent for the learning-layer argument.
- **Probability transformation**: Prelec (1998); Tversky & Kahneman (1992);
  description–experience gap (Hertwig et al. 2004; Ungemach et al. 2009;
  Wulff et al. 2018) — cited as a distinct mechanism, not conflated with the
  Prelec scenario.
- **Reputation**: Kreps & Wilson (1982); Dranove & Satterthwaite (2000).
- **Forecast/verification scoring**: Murphy (1993); Cox (1972) hazards;
  Hersbach et al. (2019, ERA5).
- **Known-zero benchmark / institutional plausibility**: Espín-Sánchez &
  Gil-Guirado (2022); Espín-Sánchez et al. (2026); Akong'a (1987); Skinner
  (1948); environmental valuation (Pattanayak & Kramer 2001; Hensher et al.
  2006).

## What the manuscript does not cite itself as

- Not a behavioral-estimation paper (Prelec is a parameterized scenario).
