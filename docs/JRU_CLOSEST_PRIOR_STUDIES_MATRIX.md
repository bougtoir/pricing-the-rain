## Verdict

Each building block is established: endogenous sampling biases experience
(1–4, 7, 9, 10), selected-sample means equal one plus a scaled covariance
(8), probability weighting and the divergence of Prelec weights relative to
\(p\) (11), and selection-driven provider ranking distortion (14). None of
the closest studies puts risk-correlated observation, subjective
transformation, and an additive reputation stock in one reduced-form quote
and asks how the composition of that quote changes with rarity when the
counterfactual is hidden. None checks the mechanism against a real
stochastic process with known zero efficacy. These two items are the only
candidates for incremental novelty (see `docs/JRU_NOVELTY_GATE.md`).
