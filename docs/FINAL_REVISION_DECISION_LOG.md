# Final revision decision log

## 2026-09-24: retain the existing project and exact-zero design

The revision continues the existing canonical project. It does not change the archived
weather, eight pre-specified locations, training/evaluation split, 2,595 pseudo-events,
M0-M5 structure, principal replications, seeds, robustness grid, or permutation
algorithm unless a source defect is found. The equality
\(R_{rt}(1)=R_{rt}(0)=R_{rt}\) remains imposed.

## 2026-09-24: narrow novelty after full QJE comparison

The QJE article already establishes ineffective rain prayer, endogenous drought timing,
increasing-hazard persuasion, rainfall demand, tangible benefits and support costs,
historical offerings and payments, cultural selection, persistence, and a
strategic-timing alternative. These are context rather than new claims.

The revised contribution is:

1. request-to-acceptance provider selection with rejected requests;
2. multiple provider-specific reputation stocks and prices;
3. selective/recency memory;
4. explicit transfer, cost, revenue, profit, surplus, and plural-value distinctions;
5. stock-flow decoupling between past attributed performance and current ecological
   success;
6. risk-adjusted environmental-provider performance.

## 2026-09-24: title

Use:

> **Pricing Apparent Environmental Performance: Strategic Selection and Reputation

This removes “Environmental Valuation,” which could imply empirical valuation, and

## 2026-09-24: article category

Retain **Analysis**. The paper analyzes an important ecological-economic evaluation
problem using observed-weather pseudo-events and a calibrated model. The live limit is
8,000 words or 25 double-spaced pages.

## 2026-09-24: failed price-success hypothesis

Do not retune, hide, or subordinate the unfavorable result. The positive association is
not supported by two-way clustered inference, the within-stratum falsification is
negative, and M4-M5 event-level differences are negative. This becomes a principal
finding in the abstract, results, Figure 4, discussion, conclusion, highlights, and
cover letter.

## 2026-09-24: stock versus flow

Use the model-supported formulation:

- past attributed outcomes update a provider-specific reputation stock;
- current reputation and current hazard determine perceived incremental efficacy and
  the simulated quote;
- current apparent success is a flow outcome from natural rainfall under the current
  hazard;
- therefore a reputation premium need not imply a positive contemporaneous
  price-success association.

This is a calibrated mechanism result, not an empirical historical law.

## 2026-09-24: valuation terminology

Classify monetary and value terms as observed, calibrated scenario, simulated
endogenous, theoretical, historical evidence, transfer, resource cost, or
non-monetary/social value. Preserve:

> economic value of rainfall
> != causal value of intervention-created rainfall
> != scenario WTP
> != provider revenue-maximizing price
> != expenditure transfer
> != resource cost
> != social/relational/cultural value.

No scenario unit will be labeled as real currency or a historical estimate.

## 2026-09-24: M0-M5 interpretation

Use “staged model comparison,” “mechanism decomposition,” and “sequential generative
enrichment,” not one-factor causal decomposition. State that M0-M1 permute outcomes
within the sampled year and M2-M5 restore observed local and seasonal ordering before
adding acceptance and observer mechanisms.

## 2026-09-24: historical and cultural scope

Historical evidence supports requests, offerings, collections, specialized
practitioners, and context-specific payments. It does not establish a universal
drought-contingent fee schedule or the calibrated provider strategy. All modeled
events remain fictional, and the analysis does not reduce cultural value to monetary
utility.

## 2026-09-24: exploratory permutation

Retain the negative permutation result as a falsification diagnostic. Report the
strata, within-stratum demeaning and shuffling, covariate adjustment, 1,000
permutations, and exchangeability limitation. Do not present \(p\approx0.002\) as
confirmatory historical evidence.
