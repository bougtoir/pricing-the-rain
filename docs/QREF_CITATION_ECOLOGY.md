# QREF citation ecology audit

Date: 2026-10-18. Scope: positioning the manuscript as an economics paper
for *The Quarterly Review of Economics and Finance* (QREF, Elsevier;
journal of the Midwest Economic Association). QREF publishes theoretical,
empirical, and policy work in economics, financial economics, and finance.

## Why this is a QREF economics paper and not a rainmaking/environmental paper

- The objects are economic: a reduced-form posted quote, a price-implied
  index, a reputation stock, and a selection decomposition — not a physical
  process. The rainmaking benchmark is a *known-zero control*, not the
  subject of study; the observed-weather layer supplies a real stochastic
  hazard under which selection produces measurable apparent-performance
  differences, and the model-overlay layer is explicitly labeled
  calibrated simulation.
- The core results are comparative statics about quote composition under
  endogenous observation — the same family of questions QREF publishes on
  selection, reputation, and behavioral decision-making (see below).
