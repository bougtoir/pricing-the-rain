# Economic Modelling fit audit

- Journal: Economic Modelling (Elsevier), ISSN 0264-9993. Hybrid subscription
  journal; open access optional (Elsevier APC applies only if the author opts in) —
  satisfies the no-mandatory-APC constraint. JCR Q1 Economics; JIF ≈ 4.7 (2024 JCR).
- Fit: the paper is a formal + simulation framework (probability-layer separation,
  selection, probability weighting, reputation) with a controlled empirical
  validation — a standard Economic Modelling shape (theoretical model plus
  quantitative experiments plus a stylized empirical application).
- Method-type match: agent/learning models, misspecified-feedback equilibria
  (Berk–Nash), and behavioural probability weighting all appear regularly in this
  journal's neighborhood; the simulation-first design is not exotic there.
- Empirical-content match: the rainfall validation is a constructed benchmark on
  real environmental data, presented honestly as a known-zero-effect validation,
  not as a historical estimate — consistent with the journal's tolerance for
  calibrated illustrations attached to formal work.
- Title/abstract: rainmaking does not dominate the title; the general problem leads.
- Backup: Journal of Risk and Uncertainty (Springer, hybrid Open Choice; JIF ≈ 2.2;
  Scopus Q1, JCR ~Q2) — fits the probability-weighting and insurance content but is
  weaker on JCR Q1 status.
- Excluded: SAGE, MDPI, Hindawi per constraints; fully-OA journals with mandatory
  APC per constraints.
