# Phase 1 report: journal fit, literature, novelty, and data

## Outcome

**MODIFY AND GO.** The original timing-only premise overlaps substantially with
Espín-Sánchez, Gil-Guirado, and Ryan (2026). The project continues with an expanded,
explicitly ecological-economic contribution integrating environmental hazard,
endogenous demand, provider selection, plural valuation, price formation,
counterfactual attribution, and reputation under an exact zero-effect null.

## Journal

The live *Ecological Economics* scope and author guidance were retrieved and archived.
The **Analysis** category is selected, with an 8,000-word maximum, 250-word abstract,
1–7 keywords, and required 3–5 submission highlights.

## Data design

The empirical component uses archived daily precipitation responses for eight
pre-specified locations spanning contrasting rainfall regimes. Training
(1991–2010) and evaluation (2011–2020) periods are separated to prevent future
rainfall from entering prospective timing rules.

## Main limitations established before analysis

- Locations are mechanism stress tests, not historical ritual sites.
- ERA5-family point reanalysis is not station-level ground truth.
- Historical payment escalation is not assumed.
- Price and social-utility quantities are calibrated scenarios, not recovered history.
- Cultural meaning is not inferred from simulation.
