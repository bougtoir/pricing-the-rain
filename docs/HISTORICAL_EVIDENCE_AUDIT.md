# Historical-evidence audit

## Audit question

Which parts of the selection–valuation–reputation framework are historically
documented, and which are deliberately theoretical?

## Evidence trace

| Evidence | Supported use | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| Espín-Sánchez, Gil-Guirado, and Ryan, “Praying for Rain” | Endogenous timing and changing natural rainfall hazards can make prayer appear persuasive; Murcia provides the closest historical comparator. | Endogenous timing is not claimed as this paper's novelty, and the comparator does not establish this paper's simulated pricing mechanism. |
| Murcia resilience and social-stability study | Historical rain-prayer institutions can be discussed in their social and ecological context. | The paper does not transfer Murcia estimates to the eight weather locations. |
| Akong'a (1987) institutional article | Rainmaking practices were heterogeneous and culturally embedded in Kenyan cases. | The simulation is not presented as a reconstruction of Kenyan beliefs, utilities, prices, or provider strategies. |
| Pedi rainmaking study | A context-specific account documents a specialist and a stated price. | One account does not identify a universal fee schedule, drought elasticity, market structure, or success premium. |
| Plural-, shared-, and relational-value literature | Social, cultural, coordination, and relational value must remain distinct from monetary WTP and meteorological efficacy. | The calibrated social-utility term is not an empirical valuation of any historical community. |
| Drought-valuation literature | Rainfall scarcity can have economic consequences and can motivate stated-preference valuation. | Published drought valuations are not imported as historical ritual prices or as estimates of the model's scenario WTP. |

## Source integrity

Crossref metadata were archived for 27 configured DOI records. The Akong'a article was
verified against a preserved Kyoto University institutional PDF because Crossref did
not return the record. The repository landing page labels the article “A Comprehensive
Study,” whereas the PDF title page says “A Comparative Study”; the manuscript follows
the primary PDF and records the discrepancy.

Three DOI–role mismatches found during the audit are excluded in
`config/references.yml`, with verified replacements used for the intended claims. No
historical price, belief, efficacy, or provider-selection estimate was invented to fill
an evidentiary gap.

## Historical-evidence verdict

Historical sources support the existence of drought-associated ritual practices,
specialists, offerings, compensation in at least one context, and non-instrumental
social meaning. They do not identify the calibrated WTP function, the four-provider
market, the strategic acceptance rule, the Bayesian observer, or the
selective/recency-memory observer. Those elements are explicitly theoretical scenarios.
