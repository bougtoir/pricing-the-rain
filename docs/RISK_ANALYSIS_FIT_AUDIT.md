## Journal requirements (per official guidelines)

- Article type: **Original Research** (empirical; <7,000 words excluding
  references, tables, figures). Perspectives (<3,500 words) not appropriate here.
- Review model: **double-blind** — title only on page 1, abstract (≤250 words) +
  3–5 keywords on page 2, main text from page 3; no author names/affiliations in
  the manuscript file. A separate title page file is required.
- Style: double-spaced, ≥1 in margins, 8.5"×11"; numbered headings (bold caps
  main, bold subheading, italic sub-subheading); REFERENCES before tables/figures;
  figures/tables numbered and may be inline or on separate pages.
- References: **APA style** (author–date, alphabetical). The Ecological
  Economics numeric style must be converted.
- Figures: EPS/PDF/TIFF post-acceptance; keep current PDF+600-dpi PNG set.
- Declarations: data availability statement encouraged (public repo + DOI
  citation); generative-AI use must be described in Methods or Acknowledgements
  per COPE; iThenticate screening; hybrid OA optional (subscription route fine).
- Submission platform: Research Exchange (wiley.atyponrex.com/journal/RISA).
- No highlights or graphical abstract required (EE-specific items removed).

## Scope fit (SRA description)

Main focus: public health, safety and environmental risk analysis — risk
assessment, modeling and model validation, risk perception, risk communication,
risk-management decision-making and policy evaluation; cross-disciplinary
methods on real-world HS&E problems; broad-method applicability is favored.

## Fit assessment

This manuscript's general problem — how apparent provider performance should be
interpreted when baseline environmental hazard is heterogeneous, exposure is
selective, and the untreated counterfactual is unobserved — maps onto risk
characterization, performance benchmarking, and decision-making under
uncertainty. The exact zero-effect rainmaking world is a known-ground-truth
stress test, not the object of study. The model/validation emphasis (frozen
M0–M5 simulation + observed-weather ERA5 component + permutation falsification)
matches the journal's modeling area. Framing must lead with heterogeneous
baseline risk, selective exposure, and risk-adjusted performance evaluation —
not valuation or ecological economics.
