# Author actions required before submission (Journal of Risk and Uncertainty)

The scientific and reproducibility package may be finalized while these items remain
open, but the manuscript must not be submitted until the responsible author supplies
or confirms each item.

1. Confirm author names, order, affiliations, correspondence address, email, telephone
   number, and ORCIDs. The title page (`title_page.docx`) is a separate file and the
   manuscript file carries no author block.
2. Provide acknowledgements or confirm that none are required.
3. **Funding:** identify all funding sources and grant numbers, or confirm that no
   specific funding was received (Statements and Declarations, "Funding").
4. **Competing interests:** provide the declaration, or state that none exist.
5. **Author contributions:** assign CRediT roles to every author.
6. **Originality:** confirm that the manuscript is original, has not been published,
   and is not under consideration elsewhere, and that all authors approved it.
7. **AI disclosure:** Springer Nature policy — an LLM cannot be listed as an author;
   use beyond AI-assisted copy editing must be documented in the Methods or a
   suitable section. Review the Generative AI disclosure in Statements and
   Declarations and confirm final human review and responsibility.
8. **Fees:** JRU charges no submission fee. Publication is hybrid: the subscription
   route has no article processing charge; Open Choice (open access) carries an APC
   (shown on the journal page at retrieval as GBP 2290 / USD 3190 / EUR 2590; check the
   current amount and any institutional agreement). Choose a route.
9. In the Springer submission system: select the original-research article type,
   upload the blinded manuscript, separate title page, figure files (PDF/PNG),
   Online Resource 1 (supplement), and confirm the Data Availability Statement.
   JEL codes (D81, D82, D83, D84) are on the title page; confirm or adjust them.
10. Review the cover letter, select suggested or opposed reviewers if desired, and
    approve the system-generated PDF.
11. **ERA5 (resolved 2026-11-11):** by author decision ERA5 data are not
    redistributed. The public repository provides the acquisition ledgers and code,
    and replicators re-acquire the series from Open-Meteo. No further action needed.
    This replaces the earlier item asking you to confirm the legal basis for
    redistribution.
12. **Model D reputation timing (design note):** reputation enters the model D quote
    only in periods whose case is observed for that provider; otherwise the D quote
    equals the C quote. This is the existing implementation, now stated in Methods;
    confirm it is the intended specification.

Author-specific information must replace the bracketed placeholders in the title page,
manuscript declarations, cover letter, and submission system.
