# Author actions required before submission

The scientific and reproducibility package may be finalized while these items remain
open, but the manuscript must not be submitted until the responsible author supplies
or confirms each item.

1. Confirm author names, order, affiliations, correspondence address, email, telephone
   number, and ORCIDs where applicable.
2. Provide acknowledgements or explicitly confirm that none are required.
3. Identify all funding sources and grant numbers, or confirm that no specific funding
   supported the work.
4. Provide the competing-interests declaration.
5. Assign CRediT roles to every author.
6. Confirm originality and exclusive submission.
7. Confirm that every author approved the final manuscript and submission.
8. Review the factual generative-AI declaration and confirm final human review,
   editing, and responsibility for all submitted content.
9. Select the journal article type and confirm the data/code-availability responses in
   the submission system.
10. Review the cover letter, select any suggested or opposed reviewers, and approve
    the journal-generated submission PDF.

Author-specific information must replace the bracketed placeholders in the title page,
manuscript declarations, cover letter, and submission system.
