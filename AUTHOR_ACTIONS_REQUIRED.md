# Author actions required before submission (Risk Analysis)

The scientific and reproducibility package may be finalized while these items remain
open, but the manuscript must not be submitted until the responsible author supplies
or confirms each item.

1. Confirm author names, order, affiliations, correspondence address, email, telephone
   number, and ORCIDs where applicable. (Risk Analysis is double-blind: keep all of
   this on the separate title page and in the submission system only — the blinded
   manuscript must carry no names or affiliations.)
2. Provide acknowledgements or explicitly confirm that none are required.
3. Identify all funding sources and grant numbers, or confirm that no specific funding
   supported the work.
4. Provide the competing-interests declaration.
5. Assign CRediT roles to every author.
6. Confirm originality and exclusive submission (a submission is a representation that
   the work is not under consideration elsewhere).
7. Confirm that every author approved the final manuscript and submission.
8. Review the generative-AI disclosure (COPE/Wiley wording, currently in the
   Declarations section) and confirm final human review, editing, and responsibility.
9. In the Research Exchange submission (https://wiley.atyponrex.com/journal/RISA):
   select "Original Research", upload the blinded manuscript, separate title page,
   figure files (PDF/TIFF acceptable), supplementary material, and confirm the
   data-availability responses.
10. Review the cover letter, select any suggested or opposed reviewers, decide the
    hybrid open-access option, and approve the journal-generated submission PDF.

Author-specific information must replace the bracketed placeholders in the title page,
manuscript declarations, cover letter, and submission system.
